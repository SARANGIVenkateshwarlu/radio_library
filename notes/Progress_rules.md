

Here’s a focused, trackable 10‑rule plan tailored to learning Cantonese with radio/audio clips, Jyutping, and English meanings, plus daily/weekly metrics you can actually follow and evaluate.

## 1) Lock in Jyutping + tones first (Weeks 1–2)

- **Goal:** Be able to read any Jyutping syllable and identify all 6 tones in isolation and in simple words.
- **Daily (20–30 min):**
    - 10 min: Jyutping chart drill (initials, finals, tone numbers).
    - 10 min: Tone minimal pairs (e.g. si1/si2/si3/si4/si5/si6).
    - 5–10 min: Read aloud short Jyutping lines from a beginner podcast transcript.
- **Measurable checks:**
    - End of Week 1: 90% correct on a 30‑item Jyutping reading quiz (self‑made or from a site).
    - End of Week 2: 80% correct on a 20‑item tone discrimination test (listen → pick tone number).
- **Track:** Log score + time spent in a simple spreadsheet or notebook.


## 2) Use one beginner audio source with Jyutping transcripts

Pick **one** primary resource that gives: audio + Jyutping + English. Examples: Bite Size Cantonese, Chatty Cantonese, CantoneseClass101, Inspiring Cantonese.[^1][^2][^3]

- **Rule:** No hopping between 5 resources; go deep on one for 8–12 weeks.
- **Daily (20–30 min active):**
    - 1 episode (3–8 min).
    - Listen once without text → note % understood.
    - Listen with Jyutping transcript → underline unknown words.
    - Write English meaning + Jyutping for 5–10 new items.
- **Measurable:**
    - Episodes completed per week (target: 5–7).
    - New vocabulary items logged per week (target: 35–50).


## 3) Daily “clip routine” with radio/podcast segments

Use short clips (30–90 seconds) from Cantonese radio/podcasts (e.g. RTHK news intros, MTR announcements, podcast intros).[^4][^5]

- **Daily (15–20 min):**
    - Choose 1 clip.
    - Transcribe 10–20 seconds in Jyutping (pause/replay).
    - Check against transcript (if available) or ask a tutor/AI to verify.
    - Shadow (repeat aloud) the same 10–20 seconds 5–10 times.
- **Measurable:**
    - Clips processed per week: 5–7.
    - Accuracy: % of syllables you transcribed correctly (aim to increase weekly).


## 4) Build a personal “phrase bank” with Jyutping + English

- **Tool:** Anki / Quizlet / a simple CSV or Google Sheet.
- **Daily (10–15 min):**
    - Add 5–10 new phrases/sentences from your clip or episode.
    - Fields: Jyutping, English, audio link (or your own recording), topic tag.
- **Measurable:**
    - New cards/week: 35–50.
    - Review streak: days with ≥10 reviews (target: 6–7 days/week).


## 5) Shadowing + recording for pronunciation

- **Daily (10 min):**
    - Pick 2–3 sentences from your clip.
    - Listen → pause → repeat, matching tone contour and speed.
    - Record yourself on phone; compare waveform/pitch by ear.
- **Weekly metric:**
    - 3 recordings/week saved with date + source sentence.
    - Self‑rate clarity 1–5; aim for average rating to rise over 4 weeks.


## 6) Listening comprehension score each week

Once a week, do a **standardized mini‑test** using the same type of material.

- **Method (15–20 min):**
    - Play a 60–90 second clip you haven’t studied.
    - Write in English what you understood (no pause).
    - Count: number of correct ideas / total main ideas (e.g. 6/10 = 60%).
- **Track:**
    - Weekly % score in a table.
    - Target: +5–10% improvement over 4–6 weeks.


## 7) Speaking micro‑goals: “30‑second monologue”

- **3×/week (5–10 min each):**
    - Choose a simple topic (e.g. “my day”, “what I ate”, “my route to work”).
    - Prepare 4–6 sentences in Jyutping + English.
    - Record a 30‑second monologue in Cantonese (no reading while speaking if possible).
- **Metrics:**
    - Monologues/week: 3.
    - Over 4 weeks, track: average length (seconds), number of pauses, self‑rated fluency (1–5).


## 8) Weekly “radio challenge” with fixed difficulty

Pick one slightly harder radio segment per week (e.g. a 2–3 minute news intro).[^6][^4]

- **Steps (30–40 min once/week):**
    - First listen: write how much you understand (%).
    - Second pass: transcribe 20–30 seconds in Jyutping.
    - Look up unknown words; add 5–10 to your phrase bank.
    - Shadow that 20–30 seconds until smooth.
- **Metrics:**
    - Initial comprehension % each week.
    - Number of new words added from the challenge (target: 5–10/week).


## 9) Time‑boxed daily/weekly schedule (make it routine)

Design a realistic, repeatable schedule so progress is measurable by time, not just “I studied”. Example:

- **Weekdays (45–60 min/day):**
    - 20–30 min: episode + transcript (Rule 2).
    - 15–20 min: clip transcription + shadowing (Rule 3).
    - 10–15 min: Anki/phrase bank (Rule 4).
- **Weekend (60–90 min total):**
    - 1 radio challenge (Rule 8).
    - 2–3 speaking monologues (Rule 7).
- **Metrics:**
    - Days/week with ≥45 min study (target: 5–6).
    - Total minutes/week (target: 300–360).


## 10) Monthly review + adjust targets

At the end of every 4 weeks:

- **Collect:**
    - Total episodes completed.
    - Total new phrases/words added.
    - Average weekly listening % score.
    - Number of speaking recordings.
    - Total study hours.
- **Decide:**
    - Increase difficulty (longer clips, faster speech).
    - Adjust targets (e.g. from 5 to 7 episodes/week).
- **Success criteria examples:**
    - +10–15% listening comprehension vs. month start.
    - +150–250 new phrases in your bank.
    - Able to speak 60–90 seconds on simple topics with fewer pauses.

### Simple tracking template (you can copy into a sheet)

**Daily log columns:**

- Date
- Minutes studied
- Episode (title + \#)
- Clip source + length
- New phrases added
- Anki reviews done (Y/N)
- Shadowing done (Y/N)
- Self‑rating: listening (1–5), speaking (1–5)

**Weekly summary:**

- Episodes completed
- Clips processed
- New phrases total
- Listening test %
- Speaking recordings count
- Total minutes
