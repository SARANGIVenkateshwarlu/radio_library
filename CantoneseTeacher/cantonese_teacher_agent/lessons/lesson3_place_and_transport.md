# Cantonese Lesson 3: Places and Transport 地方與交通

## Lesson Overview

- **Theme:** Places and transport 地方與交通
- **Level:** L3 / beginner-intermediate
- **Main situation:** Two friends meet on the street, talk about where they are going in Tsim Sha Tsui, and decide how to travel there
- **Learning goals:**
  - Ask where someone is going and what they are doing there
  - Ask and say where a place is (exit, street, opposite a landmark)
  - Discuss transport choices (MTR, ferry, walking)
  - Say what you plan to do and give simple advice
- **Key grammar:** 諗住 / 諗緊 (planning to), 定係 (or, in questions), 梗係……啦 (of course), 而家 (now)
- **Key vocabulary:** 港鐵, 搭, 行路, 出口, 對面, 近, 而家, 梗係

---

## Dialogue Context

Peter and Amy bump into each other on the street in Hong Kong. Amy is going to Tsim Sha Tsui for afternoon tea with a friend, but she is not sure which MTR exit is closest to the restaurant. Peter helps her, then talks about his own plan: visiting the Hong Kong Museum of Art and taking the Star Ferry to Central at night. They discuss whether to take the MTR or walk. This kind of everyday exchange — greetings, directions, and transport choices — is one of the most useful conversations you can have in Hong Kong.

## Key Grammar

### 諗住 / 諗緊 + Verb

**Meaning:**
Use **諗住** (nam2 zyu6) to say you are planning or intending to do something. **諗緊** (nam2 gan2) is the continuous form: "I am (currently) thinking about it / still deciding."

**Pattern:**
`Subject + 諗住 + Verb / Verb Phrase` (a settled plan)
`Subject + 諗緊 + A 定係 B` (still deciding between A and B)

**Example 1:**
我諗住搭港鐵。
*ngo5 nam2 zyu6 daap3 gong2 tit3.*
I am planning to take the MTR.

**Example 2:**
我諗緊搭港鐵定係行路。
*ngo5 nam2 gan2 daap3 gong2 tit3 ding6 hai6 haang4 lou6.*
I'm thinking about whether to take the MTR or walk.

**Common note:**
諗住 describes a plan, but it does not guarantee the action will happen. Learners often confuse 諗住 (planning) with 諗緊 (still deciding). If you have already decided, say 我決定咗… instead.

### 定係 — "or" in questions

**Meaning:**
Use **定係** (ding6 hai6) to offer two choices inside a question: "A or B?"

**Pattern:**
`A + 定係 + B + (呀/㗎)?`

**Example 1:**
你搭港鐵定係行路？
*nei5 daap3 gong2 tit3 ding6 hai6 haang4 lou6?*
Are you taking the MTR or walking?

**Example 2:**
你想食粥定係食漢堡包呀？
*nei5 soeng2 sik6 zuk1 ding6 hai6 sik6 hon3 bou2 baau1 aa3?*
Do you want to eat congee or a hamburger?

**Common note:**
定係 is only for questions. In a statement meaning "or", use 或者 (waak6 ze2): 可以用八達通或者信用卡。 Never use 定係 in a statement.

### 梗係……啦 — "of course"

**Meaning:**
Use **梗係……啦** (gang2 hai6 … laa1) to give a strong, obvious answer: "of course…", "surely…".

**Pattern:**
`梗係 + Verb Phrase + 啦`

**Example 1:**
梗係搭港鐵啦。
*gang2 hai6 daap3 gong2 tit3 laa1.*
Of course, take the MTR.

**Example 2:**
咁遠，梗係搭的士啦。
*gam3 jyun5, gang2 hai6 daap3 dik1 si2 laa1.*
It's so far — of course take a taxi.

**Common note:**
梗係 sounds confident and friendly. It is very common in spoken Hong Kong Cantonese, but too strong for formal situations with strangers or elders you don't know well.

### 而家 — "now"

**Meaning:**
**而家** (ji4 gaa1) means "now" in spoken Cantonese. The formal written equivalent is 現在 (jin6 zoi6), which you will see on signs and in news but rarely hear in conversation.

**Pattern:**
`而家 + Verb Phrase` or `Subject + 而家 + Verb Phrase`

**Example 1:**
我而家要去尖沙咀。
*ngo5 ji4 gaa1 jiu3 heoi3 zim1 saa1 zeoi2.*
I have to go to Tsim Sha Tsui now.

**Example 2:**
而家咁熱，梗係搭港鐵啦。
*ji4 gaa1 gam3 jit6, gang2 hai6 daap3 gong2 tit3 laa1.*
It's so hot now — of course take the MTR.

**Common note:**
In fast speech 而家 is often shortened to sound like "依家" (ji1 gaa1). Both are understood; write 而家.

## Pronunciation Focus

| Item | Jyutping | Pronunciation note |
|---|---|---|
| 諗住 | nam2 zyu6 | Keep the final "m" sound in 諗 clear: na**m**2, not nan2. |
| 港鐵 | gong2 tit3 | 港 has tone 2 (rising); 鐵 has tone 3 (flat, mid). |
| 行路 | haang4 lou6 | 行 is pronounced haang4 when it means "to walk" (not hang4). |
| 出口 | ceot1 hau2 | Both syllables end with a stopped final (-t, then open). Keep 出 short and crisp. |
| 梗係 | gang2 hai6 | 梗 is tone 2 — don't flatten it into gang3. |
| 尖沙咀 | zim1 saa1 zeoi2 | All three syllables are low-flat/low tones; keep 咀 (zeoi2) rising. |

## Cultural or Local Note

> **Hong Kong note:** The Star Ferry (天星小輪) between Tsim Sha Tsui and Central/Wan Chai has been running since 1888 and is one of the cheapest and most scenic harbour crossings — pay with your Octopus card at the turnstile. Inside MTR stations, exits are labelled with letters (A1, B1, C…), so when you arrange to meet someone, Hong Kongers often say the exact exit, e.g. 「B1出口等」 "meet at exit B1".

### C. Translation

**1. I am planning to take the MTR.**
我諗住搭港鐵。
*ngo5 nam2 zyu6 daap3 gong2 tit3.*
I am planning to take the MTR.

**2. Are you taking a bus or walking?**
你搭巴士定係行路呀？
*nei5 daap3 baa1 si2 ding6 hai6 haang4 lou6 aa3?*
Are you taking a bus or walking?

**3. The restaurant is opposite the museum.**
間餐廳喺博物館對面。
*gaan1 caan1 teng1 hai2 bok3 mat6 gun2 deoi3 min6.*
The restaurant is opposite the museum.
(A natural alternative is 餐廳對面係博物館 — the museum is opposite the restaurant.)

**4. Of course, take the ferry.**
梗係搭船啦。
*gang2 hai6 daap3 syun4 laa1.*
Of course, take the ferry.
(For the Star Ferry specifically: 梗係搭天星小輪啦。)

**5. It is very hot now.**
而家好熱。
*ji4 gaa1 hou2 jit6.*
It is very hot now.
